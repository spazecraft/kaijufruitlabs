PRACTICE SAFETY

Stop what you are doing. This is practice, not a real alert.

If you ever feel the ground shake where you are:

  1. DROP where you are to your hands and knees.
  2. COVER your head and neck with your arms. Get under a sturdy desk or table if one is close.
  3. HOLD ON until the shaking stops.

Outside? Move away from buildings, power lines and anything overhead, but only if you can safely do so. Otherwise stay where you are.

Steps from NIST and the Great ShakeOut.
