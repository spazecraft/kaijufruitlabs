DOE Mandated Subjects: Coverage Ring
Each slice is one required subject. For grades 7–8 and 9–12, slice targets are the ACTUAL unit weights from Section 100.10(e): English, core content, and math carry more weight than Art or Music, because the regulation does too, converted to a per-quarter pace. Subjects the regulation requires only "on a regular basis" with no set hour count (PE, Health, Practical Arts, Library Skills, and all-grade civic/safety subjects) show as covered vs. not-covered instead of a made-up percentage. K–6 has no per-subject unit split in the regulation at all, so those always show covered/not-covered too. Tap any slice for the exact numbers and its source citation.
