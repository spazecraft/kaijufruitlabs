## KAIJU FORGE v2.1 (src/forge2.js; chibis in src/kf3d.js)
Tabs: BUILD · SKIN · THE CREW · X-RAY · MOVES.
- **BUILD, 1 · DRAW:** 20 × 24 unit grid (1 unit = 1 cm). FREEHAND snaps a closed stroke to the closest shape (circle, oval, square, rectangle, triangle, hexagon, polygon 3 to 12, star). Shape tools: circle, oval, square, rectangle, triangle, hexagon, polygon (sides slider), trapezoid (top width slider), star. Tap a shape to pick it, drag to move it, width/height sliders. Corner curve: tap a corner dot, slide; CURVE ALL CORNERS / SHARP CORNERS. TRACE (advanced, off by default): onion skin of a crew member's original art with an opacity slider.
- **BUILD, 2 · MAKE IT 3D:** every shape becomes a solid with choices (rectangle: box, cylinder, cone; circle: sphere, cylinder, cone, half-sphere; triangle: cone, pyramid, prism; hexagon/polygon: prism, pyramid, cylinder; trapezoid: cup (frustum), prism; star: prism). Part buttons: LOCK, FLIP, TURN, TILT, SIZE, DEPTH, SNAP. Gestures: 1 finger spins the whole kaiju both ways (and upside down), drag an unlocked part to move it, 2 fingers pinch, 3 fingers spin a part, double tap snaps. Locked parts never move, but the whole model always spins. View buttons: SPIN, TILT, UPSIDE DOWN, RESET VIEW. VOLUME CHECK (+12 XP per new size) and SQUARE-CUBE LAW kept from the old Forge.
- **SKIN:** pick any of the 14 crew; palette, a texture patch cut from the art (on the biggest part) and signature traits as snap-on pieces (tap a trait to take it off or put it back).
- **THE CREW:** 14 kaiju in thick-outlined circles on two-color fades, 3D chibis first (11 rendered live from the game code). Detail: spin in 3D, traits, ORIGINAL ART (bonus peek), SHAPE X-RAY, TRACE IT, SKIN MY KAIJU. SELRAH, ARTOORO and KOTOBA-KUN show their art until their 3D versions are approved side by side.
- **X-RAY:** shapes start as glowing wireframes; tap or swipe to make them solid. Shows the last shape's formula with its numbers (SHOW VOLUME reveals it) and a running total; notes that overlapping parts get counted twice.
- **MOVES:** DANCE, STOMP, WIGGLE, ROAR, HENSHIN SPIN. Every 3 active minutes unlocks the next one. Active time counts touches in the Forge, in the Sound Lab, in the JAM ON IT panel and on pads, plus STUTTER / GLITCH / WORMHOLE presses; it pauses after 30 s with no touches. DANCE reacts to the app's own sound (loudness and kick) and the PADS sit right under the stage. Saved in ST.moves {sec, n, by}.
